08 Questions, answered

Before you need to ask

A little more clarity.

Getting a new website can feel full of unknowns. These are the useful answers—the ones that make it easier to know what you’re saying yes to.

There’s no silly question.If something still feels unclear, ask me in ordinary language. I’ll answer the same way.

Ask Rebecca ↗
01Do I need my branding, photographs and copy ready first?

No. We can begin with rough notes, existing materials or simply a conversation. I’ll help you clarify the audience and wording, and if you don’t yet have a visual identity, the starter brand pack gives us a considered foundation. We’ll identify any images genuinely needed without making “get everything perfect” your first task.

02Will my website be based on a template?

No. I design and code each site around the business it belongs to. We may use familiar patterns where they help visitors—clear navigation is useful, after all—but the visual direction, structure and details won’t be a template with different colours dropped in.

03How does the monthly-fee model work?

Rather than beginning with a large design-and-build invoice, your custom website and its ongoing care sit within one monthly relationship. That covers the website, hosting, maintenance and the continuing support described above. Your exact rate and terms will be clear before anything begins—no surprise extras tucked into the process.

04Why is there no design or build fee right now?

Because Elevated Business is opening this new custom-build and ongoing-care chapter with a small group of founding clients. The £0 upfront fee rewards that early trust; it doesn’t buy a smaller website or a rushed version of the work. Once the founding period closes, these terms will close with it.

05What belongs to me—and what happens if I leave?

Your domain, business content and brand assets remain yours. If you choose to leave after six months, I’ll provide the website code at no charge, so you’re not held in a relationship that no longer suits you. The practical handover steps will be explained clearly; no awkward disappearing act and no code held hostage.

06What is included, and could anything cost extra?

The Shape it, Launch it and Care for it items above are part of the founding experience. Routine care and small changes belong in the ongoing relationship. If you later want something substantially new—such as additional functionality, a large new section or a wider rebrand—we’ll agree the scope and any cost before I touch it.

07What if the first version doesn’t feel like me?

That is exactly why you receive a private preview. You can explore the site before it becomes public and tell me what feels right or wrong. Pre-launch amends aren’t limited to an arbitrary number; we refine it until it feels like the professional version of your business—not my idea imposed on it.

08Will my new website appear on Google?

The site will be built with sound search foundations, connected to Google Analytics and supported by Google Business Profile work where relevant. No honest designer can promise a particular ranking, but I can make sure the technical starting point is strong, visible and measurable—and explain what the numbers mean afterwards.

09How quickly can the website go live?

The focused build is covered by a go-live-in-a-week guarantee once our groundwork is complete and the materials we’ve agreed are ready. That speed comes from a guided, concentrated process—not from skipping the thinking or asking you to spend the week chasing loose ends.
