01 Why this scorecard

A good business can still be harder to find, trust or buy from than it should be.

You might be excellent at what you do and still find that:

  • too much new business depends on referrals or word of mouth
  • potential clients struggle to quickly understand what you offer
  • your online presence no longer reflects the quality of your work
  • you spend too much time personally explaining and reassuring
  • interested people disappear when they are not ready to buy immediately
  • your marketing creates activity without enough meaningful enquiries

The Business Elevation Scorecard is designed for service-based businesses that want a clearer view of what is working and where opportunities may be slipping through the gaps.

It examines the journey a potential client takes from first discovering your business to deciding whether to trust you and take action.
