Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Still wondering whether virtual support is right for you? Here are the questions I hear most often.

Ask me something else →
How many hours do I need to get started?

There's no minimum. You can start with just five hours a month on an ad-hoc basis. Most clients find ten hours is the sweet spot—enough to make a real difference without overwhelming the onboarding process.

What if I don't know what to outsource?

That's very common. During our first call, we'll audit your week, identify what drains your energy, and choose the best tasks to hand over. We'll start small and adjust as we learn your rhythms.

How long is the commitment?

Ad-hoc work has no commitment. Retainers are month-to-month, so you can scale up or down as your business needs change. There are no long-term contracts unless you specifically want one.

How do you stay organised across multiple clients?

Systems are everything. I use task management tools, clear communication channels, and shared document spaces. Everything is tracked, colour-coded, and visible to you.

What if I need something urgently?

Retainer clients receive priority support. Ad-hoc clients can request rush handling for urgent jobs for a small additional fee, subject to availability.

Can you integrate with my existing tools?

Absolutely. I work with Asana, Monday.com, Notion, HubSpot, Xero, Wave, Slack and most standard business tools. If you use something niche, we'll work it out together.
