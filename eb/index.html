03 How it works

A guided, one-to-one process

A website project that doesn’t become another job.

You bring the knowledge of your business. I’ll draw it out, shape it and handle the web-design part around you.

The messy version is welcome. Rough notes, half-formed ideas and “I know what I mean, but I can’t word it” are all perfectly good places to begin.

  1. 01

    A relaxed first conversation

    Tell me what you know.

    We’ll talk about your business, the people you want to reach and what isn’t working now. There’s no polished brief to prepare and no technical questions to revise for.

    • Voice notes welcome
    • No brand pack required
  2. 02

    The thinking and making

    I turn it into direction.

    I shape the structure, visual direction and words into one considered website. You won’t be left alone with a blank document, expected to sound sales-y or asked to touch a website builder.

    • Copy support built in
    • Custom design and build
  3. 03

    Your private preview

    See it. Feel it. Refine it.

    You’ll explore the finished site privately before anybody else sees it. Tell me what feels right and what needs adjusting; there’s no meter running on pre-launch amends.

    • Nothing goes live early
    • Unlimited pre-launch amends
  4. 04

    Launch, then ongoing care

    Go live—and stay looked after.

    I handle the domain set-up, final checks and launch, then show you around without handing you a list of chores. Updates, security and the small changes afterwards stay taken care of.

    • Technical launch handled
    • A real person to come back to
